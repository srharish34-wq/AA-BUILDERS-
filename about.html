Our Story

Built on drawings that get followed, not filed away.

AA Builders & Interiors is a trusted name in Chennai's construction industry, specialising in high-quality residential buildings that combine strength, beauty and functionality.

Every project runs on the same site team from foundation to handover, so the plan your architect signs off is the plan that gets built.

  • Trusted, experienced site engineers
  • Premium materials, modern designs
  • On-time project delivery, every time
  • One transparent rate per square foot

Our Mission

To deliver well-planned, quality homes through disciplined construction, transparent communication and a team that stays accountable from the first drawing to final handover.

Our Vision

To become a trusted choice for families looking to build beautiful, functional and lasting homes through a simpler, more transparent construction experience.
